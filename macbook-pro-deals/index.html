Before ordering

  • Order on the company: enter the company name and VAT ID at checkout. Amazon needs an Amazon Business account for net prices and a company invoice; check that the seller is Amazon itself.
  • No power adapter in the box (EU rule). Any USB-C PD charger works; 70 W or more charges at full speed.
  • Chip in short: M6 has 12 CPU cores, a 12-core GPU and Thunderbolt 4. M5 Pro has 15 or 18 CPU cores, a 16 or 20-core GPU, Thunderbolt 5 and more memory bandwidth. Reported Geekbench 6 multi-core: M6 about 21,400, M5 Pro 18-core about 33,600.
  • Black Friday is Friday 27 Nov. Prices can drop a bit more; Amazon sold the 14" M5 Pro for €2,098 in June 2026. Prices change daily, so check the link before ordering.
  • Left out: private sellers (no invoice), shops with no ratings or track record, and Austrian shops.
